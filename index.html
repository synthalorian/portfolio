01 About

Steel over spectacle

I'm synth (synthalorian on GitHub), a developer based in Florida and the founder of Blackshield Company — an organization for digital forensics, security tooling, an AI platform, and the Blackshield theme family.

My work runs from low-level engines to finished apps: a hot-reloading game engine in Odin, a JUCE synthesizer with VST3 and CLAP builds, a Rust AI coding harness with persistent memory, mesh-networking tools for Reticulum, and offline-first Flutter apps for Android and iOS. Much of it is local-first by design — your data stays on your machine.

Off the keyboard, I play guitar on a praise team — which is where projects like Setlist and Open Praise come from.

Open to collaborators and new roles in game tech, audio software, AI developer tooling, and security. If something here overlaps with what you're building, get in touch.

03 Modding

Mods for Skyrim

Fifteen mods for The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition, built as one series of contracts, oaths, and steel.

Every mod stands on its own, and all fifteen are designed to run together: separate records, prefixes and scripts, no vanilla record edits, and a fifteen-plugin compatibility check that loads them side by side found no conflicts. Blackshield Codex is the optional bridge that makes the first five notice each other, and the third wave notices the earlier mods at runtime without requiring any of them.

All mods on Nexus
Game
Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition 1.6 (1.5.97 works too)
Plugins
15, all ESL-flagged, so none take a full load-order slot
Requires
Base game only. No SKSE, SkyUI, DLC or Creation Club. The Codex needs the first five.
Platforms
Windows, or Linux through Proton
Status
Early beta, not yet playtested in game. Bug reports welcome on Nexus or GitHub.
